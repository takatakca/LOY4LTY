## 1. Who does what

TAKATAK Fidélité is a loyalty program that Clover merchants offer to their customers. The merchant where you make a purchase is responsible for your information within its program; TAKATAK provides the technology that runs the program and processes the information described below on the merchant's behalf.

## 2. Information we collect

To operate the program, the App collects only:

- **Your phone number** — it is the identifier of your loyalty account.
- **Your points balance** — points earned, redeemed and expired, and the history of points movements.
- **Your program purchase history** — date, merchant/location, order amount, and points earned or redeemed for that order.
- **Optional information** — only if the merchant links it to your Clover customer profile (for example, your name) or if you provide it voluntarily.

The App **does not collect** your payment card numbers: payments are processed directly by Clover. No password is created for the loyalty account.

## 3. Why we collect this information

- Create and look up your loyalty account from your phone number
- Credit your points after a paid purchase
- Apply your points redemptions at the counter or the kiosk
- Display your balance and history
- Provide the merchant with aggregate program reports (active members, points issued and redeemed)

We do **not** use your information for third-party advertising, and we **never sell** your personal information.

## 4. Consent

By entering your phone number on the loyalty screen, you consent to the creation of an account and to the processing described in this policy. You may withdraw your consent and close your account at any time by contacting the merchant or us (see Section 9); closing the account forfeits unredeemed points unless the merchant decides otherwise.

## 5. Disclosure and hosting

- Your information is processed in Clover (Fiserv) systems when the App reads your orders, payments and Clover customer profile, and in TAKATAK's hosting infrastructure for the points ledger.
- It may be hosted or processed in Canada or the United States depending on the infrastructure used; it remains protected by the measures described in Section 7 and by our providers' contractual obligations.
- We do not disclose your information to any other third party, except where required by law or with your consent.

## 6. Retention

We retain your information while your loyalty account is active. An account with no activity for 24 months may be closed and its information destroyed or anonymized, unless the merchant configures a different period. After closure, information is destroyed within a reasonable time, subject to the merchant's legal retention obligations (invoicing, taxes).

## 7. Security

We apply safeguards proportionate to the sensitivity of the information: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), restricted access to authorized personnel, authentication for administrative access, and logging. No system is infallible; if a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious harm, we will notify the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec and the individuals concerned, in accordance with Law 25.

## 8. Your rights (Law 25 and applicable laws)

You have the right to:

- Access your information and obtain a copy
- Have inaccurate or incomplete information rectified
- Withdraw your consent and request closure of your account
- Request deletion of your information, subject to the merchant's legal obligations
- Receive your information in a structured technological format (portability), where the law provides for it
- File a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec

To exercise a right, contact the merchant or our Privacy Officer (Section 9).
